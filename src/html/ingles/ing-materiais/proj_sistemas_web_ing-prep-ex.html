Prepositions - Exercícios

Resolva os exercícios abaixo para testar seus conhecimentos sobre o uso das preposições em inglês:

1. Complete: The book is ___ the table.


  • A) in
  • B) on
  • C) at
  • D) under


Resposta correta: B

2. Complete: She was born ___ July.


  • A) on
  • B) in
  • C) at
  • D) by


Resposta correta: B

3. Complete: We arrived ___ the airport at 6 p.m.


  • A) in
  • B) on
  • C) at
  • D) to


Resposta correta: C

4. Complete: The cat is hiding ___ the bed.


  • A) under
  • B) on
  • C) in
  • D) over


Resposta correta: A

5. Complete: I will meet you ___ Monday.


  • A) in
  • B) at
  • C) on
  • D) by


Resposta correta: C

6. Complete: He walked ___ the room and sat down.


  • A) into
  • B) onto
  • C) under
  • D) over


Resposta correta: A

7. Complete: The painting is hanging ___ the wall.


  • A) in
  • B) on
  • C) at
  • D) over


Resposta correta: B

8. Complete: She lives ___ London.


  • A) on
  • B) at
  • C) in
  • D) to


Resposta correta: C

9. Complete: The train leaves ___ 8 o’clock.


  • A) in
  • B) on
  • C) at
  • D) by


Resposta correta: C

10. Complete: The gift is ___ you.


  • A) to
  • B) for
  • C) by
  • D) with


Resposta correta: B
